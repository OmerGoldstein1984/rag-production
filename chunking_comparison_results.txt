2. Recursive strategy creates fewer, larger chunks by respecting paragraph boundaries, minimizing bad splits
3. Structure-aware strategy balances document structure with size constraints, producing good results for markdown content
4. For RAG applications, recursive or structure-aware strategies generally outperform fixed-size pure approaches
