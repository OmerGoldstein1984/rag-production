# Acme Corp Travel Policy

## Effective Date: July 1, 2024
## Version: 1.0

### Policy Overview
This policy governs business travel expenses incurred by employees of Acme Corp. All travel must be pre-authorized and expenses must be submitted for reimbursement in accordance with this policy.

### Authorization
- All domestic and international travel must be approved by the employee's manager and the Finance Department.
- Travel requests must be submitted at least 10 business days in advance for domestic travel and 15 business days for international travel.
- The travel request must include the purpose of travel, itinerary, estimated costs, and any required visas or vaccinations.

### Booking
- Employees must use the Acme Corp travel booking tool for all airfare, hotel, and car rental reservations.
- Exceptions to using the booking tool must be approved by the Travel Management Team.
- Employees should select the most cost-effective option that meets business needs.

### Expenses
#### Per Diem Rates
- Meals and incidental expenses (M&IE): $65 per day for domestic travel, $75 per day for international travel.
- Lodging: Actual hotel costs up to the maximum allowed rate for the location (see Lodging Guidelines).
- The first and last day of travel are calculated at 75% of the full daily M&IE rate.

#### Lodging Guidelines
- Maximum lodging rates vary by city and are updated quarterly.
- Employees must stay at hotels that are safe, clean, and conveniently located to business activities.
- Luxury hotels (5-star) are not permitted unless approved by the Finance Department for special circumstances.

#### Transportation
- Airfare: Economy class is required for all flights. Business class may be approved for flights over 6 hours or for medical reasons.
- Car Rentals: Midsize or equivalent vehicles are standard. Upgrades require justification and approval.
- Mileage: Personal vehicle use for business travel is reimbursed at the IRS standard mileage rate ($0.67 per mile as of 2024).
- Public Transportation: Actual costs for subways, buses, taxis, and rideshare services are reimbursable.

### Expense Reporting
- All expenses must be submitted via the Acme Corp expense reporting system within 10 business days of returning from travel.
- Original receipts are required for all expenses over $25. For expenses under $25, a receipt is recommended but not required.
- Expense reports must be approved by the employee's manager before processing for reimbursement.

### Abbreviations
- M&IE: Meals and Incidental Expenses
- IRS: Internal Revenue Service
