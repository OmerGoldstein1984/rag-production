# Acme Corp Remote Work Policy

## Effective Date: March 15, 2024
## Version: 1.2

### Policy Overview
Acme Corp allows eligible employees to work remotely under certain conditions to support work-life balance and productivity.

### Eligibility
- Employees must have completed a 6-month introductory period.
- Performance ratings must meet or exceed expectations for the last review cycle.
- Roles that require physical presence (e.g., lab technicians, customer service desk) are not eligible for remote work.

### Remote Work Arrangements
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
- Remote work must be scheduled in advance and approved by the manager.
- Core collaboration hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time) must be observed regardless of work location.
- Employees must maintain a dedicated workspace that is safe and free from distractions.

### Equipment and Expenses
- Acme Corp provides a laptop, monitor, and necessary peripherals for remote work.
- Employees are responsible for internet service costs; however, Acme Corp offers a monthly stipend of $50 for internet reimbursement.
- All equipment remains the property of Acme Corp and must be returned upon termination or when remote work ends.

### Security Requirements
- Remote employees must use the company VPN to access internal resources.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all remote logins.
- Employees must ensure their home network is secured with a strong password and encryption.

### Abbreviations
- MFA: Multi-Factor Authentication
- VPN: Virtual Private Network
