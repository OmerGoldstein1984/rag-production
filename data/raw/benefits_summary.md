# Acme Corp Benefits Summary

## Health and Wellness
- Medical Insurance: Acme Corp offers three medical plan options through major carriers, with the company covering 80% of the premium for employee-only coverage.
- Dental Insurance: Comprehensive dental plan covering preventive, basic, and major services; company pays 100% of premium for employee coverage.
- Vision Insurance: Annual eye exams, lenses, and frames covered up to specified allowances.
- Mental Health: Employee Assistance Program (EAP) provides free confidential counseling services.
- Wellness Program: Includes gym reimbursement ($30/month), smoking cessation support, and annual health screenings.

## Financial Benefits
- 401(k) Plan: Company matches 50% of employee contributions up to 6% of salary.
- Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP): Purchase company stock at a 15% discount through payroll deductions.
- Life Insurance: Company-paid term life insurance equal to 1x annual salary.
- Disability Insurance: Short-term and long-term disability coverage at no cost to employees.

## Time Off
- Paid Time Off (PTO): As outlined in the Leave Policy, employees accrue PTO based on tenure.
- Holidays: 10 paid company holidays per year.
- Parental Leave: 12 weeks of paid leave for birth, adoption, or foster care placement.
- Bereavement Leave: Up to 5 days of paid leave for the loss of an immediate family member.

## Perks and Discounts
- Commuter Benefits: Pre-tax deductions for public transportation and parking.
- Tuition Reimbursement: Up to $5,250 per year for job-related courses.
- Employee Discounts: Discounts on Acme Corp products and services.
- Referral Bonus: $1,000 for referring a candidate who is hired and stays 90 days.

### Abbreviations
- EAP: Employee Assistance Program
- ESPP: Employee Stock Purchase Plan
