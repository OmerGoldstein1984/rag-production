# Acme Corp Leadership Meeting Minutes

## Meeting Date: April 10, 2024
## Attendees: CEO, CFO, CTO, VP of Engineering, VP of Sales, VP of HR
## Prepared by: Executive Assistant

### Agenda
1. Q1 Financial Review
2. Product Launch Timeline
3. Hiring Freeze Discussion
4. IT Security Initiatives
5. Employee Benefits Update

### Discussion Points

#### 1. Q1 Financial Review
- Revenue reached $12.5M, exceeding the forecast of $11.8M.
- Gross margin improved to 68% due to cost optimization in cloud services.
- Operating expenses were 2% over budget due to unplanned marketing expenses.
- **Action Item**: CFO to investigate marketing overspend and report by April 17.

#### 2. Product Launch Timeline
- The AcmeCloud Storage Service 2.0 launch is scheduled for June 15, 2024.
- Beta testing phase begins May 1, 2024, with selected enterprise customers.
- Marketing campaign will start two weeks prior to launch.
- **Note**: The launch date was moved from June 1 to June 15 to accommodate additional security testing.
- **Action Item**: CTO to confirm security testing completion by May 20.

#### 3. Hiring Freeze Discussion
- Due to economic uncertainty, a hiring freeze is being considered for non-essential roles.
- Exceptions will be made for critical roles in security and customer support.
- The freeze would be effective May 1, 2024, and reviewed monthly.
- **Contradiction Note**: This conflicts with the recent hiring plan outlined in the Onboarding Guide, which planned for 50 new hires in Q2. The hiring freeze supersedes the onboarding plan for Q2.
- **Action Item**: VP of HR to clarify hiring guidelines for managers by April 15.

#### 4. IT Security Initiatives
- Implementation of multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all remote access is complete.
- Phishing simulation training will be conducted quarterly starting next month.
- The IT Security Team is evaluating a Zero Trust Architecture for future implementation.
- **Action Item**: CTO to present Zero Trust feasibility study by May 10.

#### 5. Employee Benefits Update
- The new dental plan provider will be effective July 1, 2024.
- The company will increase the 401(k) match to 60% of employee contributions up to 6% of salary, effective January 1, 2025.
- **Note**: The increase in 401(k) match contradicts the current Benefits Summary, which states a 50% match. The updated match will be reflected in the Benefits Summary starting January 2025.
- **Action Item**: VP of HR to update Benefits Summary and communicate changes to employees by June 15.
