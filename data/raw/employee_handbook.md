# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Welcome to Acme Corp

This handbook provides an overview of our company policies, procedures, and culture. It is designed to help you succeed in your role at Acme Corp.

### Our Mission
To innovate and deliver cutting-edge technology solutions that empower our customers to achieve their business goals.

### Our Values
- Innovation: We embrace new ideas and continuously improve our products and services.
- Integrity: We act with honesty and transparency in all our interactions.
- Collaboration: We work together as one team to achieve shared objectives.
- Excellence: We strive for the highest quality in everything we do.

### Employment Basics
- Work Hours: Standard work hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.
- Dress Code: Business casual is the standard dress code. Employees may dress more formally for client meetings.
- Payroll: Employees are paid bi-weekly via direct deposit.
- Benefits Eligibility: Full-time employees are eligible for benefits starting on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment.

### Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to:
- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect and dignity.
- Maintain confidentiality of proprietary information.
- Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose any potential conflicts.
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations.

### Intellectual Property
All inventions, discoveries, and works created by employees during the course of their employment at Acme Corp are the exclusive property of Acme Corp.
