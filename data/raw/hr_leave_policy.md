# Acme Corp Leave Policy

## Effective Date: January 1, 2024
## Version: 2.1

### Policy Overview
Acme Corp provides paid time off (PTO) to all full-time employees. PTO includes vacation, sick leave, and personal days.

### Leave Accrual
- Employees accrue PTO at a rate of 6.67 hours per month (80 hours per year) for the first 5 years of service.
- After 5 years of service, employees accrue PTO at a rate of 10 hours per month (120 hours per year).
- Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis based on their FTE.

### Leave Usage
- PTO can be used for vacation, sick leave, or personal time.
- Employees must request PTO in advance using the Acme Corp time-off system, except for unforeseen sick leave.
- Minimum increment for PTO usage is 1 hour.
- Maximum carryover from one year to the next is 40 hours. Any excess accrued beyond the carryover limit will be forfeited.

### Approval Process
- All PTO requests must be approved by the employee's manager.
- Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance for planned vacation.
- For sick leave, employees must notify their manager as soon as possible, preferably before the start of the workday.

### Additional Notes
- PTO is paid at the employee's base rate of pay.
- Unused PTO is not paid out upon termination unless required by state law.
- This policy replaces all previous versions of the Acme Corp Leave Policy.

### Abbreviations
- PTO: Paid Time Off
- FTE: Full-Time Equivalent
