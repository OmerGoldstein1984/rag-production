# Acme Corp Pricing Guide

## Effective Date: April 1, 2024

### Overview
This guide outlines the pricing structure for Acme Corp's core products and services. All prices are in USD and do not include applicable taxes.

### AcmeCloud Storage Service
**Storage Pricing (per GB per month):**
- First 50 TB: $0.023
- Next 450 TB: $0.022
- Over 500 TB: $0.021

**Request Pricing (per 1,000 requests):**
- PUT, COPY, POST, or LIST requests: $0.005
- GET and other requests: $0.0004

**Data Transfer Pricing (per GB):**
- First 10 TB/month: $0.00 (free)
- Next 40 TB/month: $0.09
- Next 100 TB/month: $0.07
- Over 150 TB/month: $0.05

### AcmeCompute Virtual Machines
**On-Demand Pricing (per hour):**
- General Purpose (t3.small): $0.0206
- General Purpose (t3.medium): $0.0412
- Compute Optimized (c5.large): $0.085
- Memory Optimized (r5.large): $0.126

**Reserved Instances:**
- 1-year term: Up to 40% discount
- 3-year term: Up to 60% discount

### AcmeDB Managed Database
**Instance Pricing (per hour):**
- db.t3.micro: $0.017
- db.t3.small: $0.034
- db.t3.medium: $0.068
- db.r5.large: $0.326

**Storage Pricing (per GB per month):**
- General Purpose SSD: $0.115
- Provisioned IOPS SSD: $0.140

### Abbreviations
- GB: Gigabyte
- TB: Terabyte
- SSD: Solid State Drive
