# Acme Corp Leave Policy (Version 1.0)

## Effective Date: January 1, 2022
## Version: 1.0
## STATUS: OBSOLETE - SEE HR_LEAVE_POLICY_VERSION_2.1.md

### Policy Overview
Acme Corp provides paid time off (PTO) to all full-time employees. PTO includes vacation, sick leave, and personal days.

### Leave Accrual
- Employees accrue PTO at a rate of 5 hours per month (60 hours per year) for all years of service.
- Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis based on their FTE.

### Leave Usage
- PTO can be used for vacation, sick leave, or personal time.
- Employees must request PTO in advance using the Acme Corp time-off system.
- Minimum increment for PTO usage is 4 hours.
- Maximum carryover from one year to the next is 20 hours.

### Approval Process
- All PTO requests must be approved by the employee's manager.
- Requests should be submitted at least one week in advance.

### Additional Notes
- PTO is paid at the employee's base rate of pay.
- Unused PTO is paid out upon termination at a rate of 50% of the accrued balance.

### Abbreviations
- PTO: Paid Time Off
- FTE: Full-Time Equivalent
